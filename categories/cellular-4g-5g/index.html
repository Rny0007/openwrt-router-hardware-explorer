📡 Mobile Broadband

Best 4G / 5G Cellular OpenWrt Routers & Mobile Gateways

For remote installations, maritime deployments, RVs, and enterprise multi-WAN failover, cellular OpenWrt routers provide carrier-independent mobile broadband without vendor cloud lock-in or subscription fees.

📁 All Guides 🏆 Best Routers Overall⚡ Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be)📶 Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)✈️ Travel & Pocket Gateways🕸️ Mesh & Whole-Home Wi-Fi🚀 Multi-Gigabit (2.5G & 10G)🛡️ x86 Mini-PC Firewalls📡 4G / 5G Cellular Gateways

Architectural Selection & Evaluation Criteria

  • Integrated Qualcomm or Quectel LTE Cat 12+ or 5G NR modem with Carrier Aggregation (CA) support.
  • Driver support for QMI, MBIM, or ModemManager / RNDIS in OpenWrt.
  • External SMA antenna connectors for external directional or MIMO cellular antennas.
  • Multi-WAN failover support (mwan3) for seamless zero-downtime switching between fixed-line and cellular uplinks.
  • Dual-SIM slot redundancy for automatic switching between different telecom providers.
