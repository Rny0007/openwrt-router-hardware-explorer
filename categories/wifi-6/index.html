Best OpenWrt Wi-Fi 6 Routers: Tested AX3000 to AX6000 Hardware

Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax) represents the current mature standard for OpenWrt deployments. MediaTek Filogic and Qualcomm IPQ platforms offer 100% open-source wireless drivers with full hardware offload.

📁 All Guides 🏆 Best Routers Overall⚡ Wi-Fi 7 (802.11be)📶 Wi-Fi 6 (802.11ax)✈️ Travel & Pocket Gateways🕸️ Mesh & Whole-Home Wi-Fi🚀 Multi-Gigabit (2.5G & 10G)🛡️ x86 Mini-PC Firewalls📡 4G / 5G Cellular Gateways

Architectural Selection & Evaluation Criteria

  • Fully open-source mac80211 kernel wireless drivers (MediaTek mt7915/mt7986 or Qualcomm ath11k).
  • True multi-gigabit routing capacity: MediaTek Filogic 830 / 820 or Qualcomm IPQ807x / IPQ60xx.
  • WPA3-Personal, WPA3-Enterprise, and 802.11w Protected Management Frames support.
  • Target Wake Time (TWT) and BSS Coloring support for dense client deployments.
  • Flashing safety: factory firmware vulnerability or unlocked U-Boot bootloader.
