  • 802.11ac (5 GHz): Chipset Realtek RTL8812FR, MIMO/Streams: N/A

Known Limitations & Operational Notes

  • CARRIER GPON LOCK & PROPRIETARY OMCI: The optical GPON transceiver and BOSA assembly are driven by closed-source Realtek optical MAC firmware.
  • Flashing non-OEM firmware breaks carrier OLT authentication (SLID / LOID / OMCI), rendering the fiber connection completely inoperable.
  • Recommendation for users: Put the A-622G into Bridge Mode (VLAN 100) and connect a supported OpenWrt router (e.g. Jio JIDU6101 or GL-MT6000) for PPPoE dialing and routing.
