TP-Link Archer AX50

Wireless Radios

  • 802.11ax (2.4 GHz): Chipset Intel WAV654 (Proprietary PCIe), MIMO/Streams: N/A
  • 802.11ax (5 GHz): Chipset Intel WAV654 (Proprietary PCIe 160MHz), MIMO/Streams: N/A

Known Limitations & Operational Notes

  • UNSUPPORTED HARDWARE ARCHITECTURE: The Intel AnyWAN GRX350 paired with Intel WAV654 PCIe Wi-Fi 6 transceivers lacks open-source mac80211 Linux drivers.
  • Intel's proprietary wireless driver stack was never released to mainline open-source or ported to OpenWrt.
  • Known severe hardware thermal issues on stock firmware; attempting to flash alternative firmware risks bricking without Wi-Fi driver recovery.
